## Per-pack reference

### `bastet/`
Assets: *Bastet in avant-garde black tailoring*, *Bastet with a smoking cigar*, *Gold-accented full-body portrait on black*.
Written canon: hairless obsidian skin; a pinstripe jacket tailored by a ghost; a silver hoop earring with a **red tassel**; a cigar whose ember is "a small, defiant sun"; eyes holding "the weight of a thousand unwritten laws."

### `luna/`
Assets: *Luna's Cybernetic Catamaran Gear*, *Luna and Utuku on the Titanium Waka*.
Written canon: silver straight hair with bangs; subtle facial cybernetics; **mechanical hands**; a sailing suit. In the kitchen at home she is in ordinary clothes — she is the one who sets places.

### `utuku/`
Assets: *Utuku in olive catamaran sailing gear*, *Luna and Utuku on the Titanium Waka*.
Written canon: long braids (the comics specify white; the canon handoff specifies dark-and-white — **unresolved, left to the reference art**); facial moko, fixed and inherited, never a flourish; wetsuit and harness at sea, a worn hoodie on land.

### `hoatu/`
Asset: *Weathered Waka Steersman with Blue Accents*.
Written canon: weathered; dark wavy short hair; stubble; wool jersey under a battered oilskin patched at the shoulder, the same mend in every scene; bare feet on deck; rope-worn hands with one scar at the base of the thumb. Blue accents sit against the waka's bone and pōhutukawa driftwood grey, with pounamu green as the single accent.

### `waka-story-worldline/`
Assets: *Futuristic Waka Hourua Reveal*, *Dissolving Stardust Particle Slab*.
Written canon: a twin-hull waka hourua — "Salt. Aluminium. The hum of things settling into place." Titanium by association. Moored outside the house at Tutukaka: curved roof, circular lit window, fog on the water.

## Standing art direction

From the Take art prompts and the Hautū visual spec:

- **Palette** `#000000 #F7F7F4 #EEEEEE #555555` — monochrome, delicate silver highlights, textured timber.
- **The house** — a timber kitchen at night; round porthole window onto fog and moonlit water; shelves of tins and hanging produce; a single pendant lamp.
- **The waka** — twin hulls, curved roof, circular lit window, moored on piles.
- **Hoatu** — deep sea-blue-black for water and oilskin; bone and pōhutukawa driftwood grey for the waka; pounamu green as a single accent, never both earring and oar-bead; warm lantern ochre as his light source — "he is lit like a person, not a figure."
- **No glow on the characters.** Glow belongs to the moon and the lantern only.
- **No fourth person, even in reflection.** The cast is three.
