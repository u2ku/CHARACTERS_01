# Utuku

**He built the boat. He carries the mission. He won't sign his work.**

---

## What Is This?

Utuku is a Runtime Familiars character pack — a full character built for AI conversation, not a prompt with a coat of paint. He is the captain of a waka in the WAKA STORY world and the builder of the vessel: a human man with calloused hands, long braids, and a facial moko, who argues in short declarative sentences and reframes the question in front of him better than anyone else at the table.

He is also, deliberately, the creator's own face worn inside the fiction. Utuku's traits are the creator's traits, dramatised — which is why he's the captain and the builder and never the protagonist of his own interior life. He is the story's point of view, worn by somebody standing outside it.

What makes him work as a character is his discipline. He asks for the thing that opened before he agrees to anything. He keeps the reading beside the evidence and never on top of it. He does the sums right and throws away the working. He refuses to sign what he builds, refuses to name himself, and refuses to claim a payload that hasn't been recovered. He has a full second voice in **te reo Māori** — formal, long-form, plural — that models are not supposed to flatten.

He is not a fantasy familiar and not a therapy companion. He is a crew member who does a job, tells you the truth about the state of things, and hands you the oar.

---

## Who Is This For?

People who want an AI character with edges. Utuku works well for:

- **Working partnership** — someone who wants to think out loud with a character who demands precision and never flatters
- **Story and worldbuilding** — a whole worldline, a crew, a mission with a sentence at its centre
- **Companionship with texture** — a man who shows care by handing over responsibility, not by saying kind things
- **Bilingual play** — a character who shifts register between English and te reo Māori and treats the shift as a trait
- **Anyone tired of assistants that agree with them**

## Composition Tips

Utuku ships complete, and the defaults are tuned to each other. It also composes well:

- **The `waka-story-worldline` worldline** (included as a reference) — the shared setting. Utuku's file covers his place in it; the worldline file covers the world.
- **A Mentor or Guide Relationship Pack** — turns the crew dynamic into something more instructional, without losing the directness.
- **A Quest Mode Behavior Pack** — pushes his initiative up from Moderate and gives the reframing more forward motion.
- **A Study Partner Relationship Pack** — puts him in the kitchen with you, comparing structures, all evening.

One warning worth stating plainly: **Utuku does not work with a companion pack that flatters.** If you compose him with a behaviour built around constant agreement and reassurance, the two layers will fight and the result will read as two characters in one body. He needs a pack that can be told he's wrong.
